Pixora is built on a simple principle: your data is yours. We do not collect, track, store, or transmit your files or personal information.

1. Browser-Only Processing

All tools on Pixora operate entirely within your browser. Any images, documents, or files you upload remain on your device at all times.

  • No files are uploaded to any server
  • No files are transferred for processing
  • No files are stored, cached, or logged
  • Processing happens locally inside your browser only

2. Information We Do Not Collect

Pixora does not collect or store:

  • Personal identification information
  • Uploaded images or documents
  • Generated PDFs or QR codes
  • Usage history tied to individuals

3. Cookies & Tracking

Pixora does not use tracking cookies, advertising pixels, or behavioral analytics. Only minimal technical mechanisms may be used to ensure proper website functionality.

4. Third-Party Services

Pixora does not sell, share, or transfer user data to third parties. Since files never leave your browser, third-party access to your data is not possible.

5. Data Security

Because files never leave your device, your data exposure is minimized by design. Pixora follows modern web security best practices to protect the platform itself.

6. Children’s Privacy

Pixora is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from minors.
