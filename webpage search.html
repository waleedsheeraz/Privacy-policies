Privacy Policy — Search Page

Last updated: November 19, 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how Search Page (the “App”, “we”, “us”) handles user information. The App is designed to process input locally on the user's device. We do not collect, store, sell, or share personal data.

1. Information We Collect

a. Information you provide:

  • Webpage URLs you enter.
  • Text, words, or numbers you type into the app.
  • Files you upload (XLSX, XLS, CSV).

All input is processed on-device to perform the search and highlighting functions.

2. Automatically Collected Information

The App does not collect device identifiers, location data, analytics, advertising IDs, or other personal information. No third-party trackers or analytics SDKs are used.

3. How We Use Your Data

Your input is used solely to:

  • Extract entries from uploaded spreadsheets.
  • Scan and highlight matches on the webpage you provide.
  • Present results and navigation (Next/Previous).

Data is not retained after the session unless you explicitly export or save it yourself.

4. Data Storage & Security

All processing occurs in-memory on your device or within the app's runtime environment. The app does not upload or back up your inputs to external servers or cloud storage. When the app is closed or refreshed, inputs are cleared from memory.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not share your data with third parties, advertisers, analytics providers, or other organizations — except to comply with valid legal requests when required by law.

6. Third-Party Content

The App loads the webpage you provide into an isolated preview (iframe/sandbox). The App does not send page content to any external service for processing. If you paste or upload content that contains third-party data, that data remains within your device session.

7. Children’s Privacy

This App is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. Updated versions will be posted in the app listing and within the app where applicable. The effective date at the top will reflect the latest revision.
